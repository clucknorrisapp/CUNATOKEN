CUNA CUMMIN' FOR YA is a catching game. A pair of lips sits at the bottom of the board and things fall from the top. Catch the tacos to score; the big taco is worth more. On a phone, tap or hold the cross or the stick in either gutter, or swipe the board; left and right slide the lips, up and down plant them. On a keyboard, hold the left and right arrows or A and D.
